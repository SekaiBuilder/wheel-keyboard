How to use it

Each thumb has a wheel with 8 directions. The inner ring holds the 16 most used letters: vowels on one hand and consonants on the other, so the thumbs take turns. The outer ring holds the rare ones and punctuation. Slide and lift; if you return to the centre before lifting, nothing is typed.

Tap without moving: Space on the consonant wheel, delete on the vowel wheel (hold to repeat). Above the wheels: ⇧, ⋯ and ⏎. Hold your thumb still on a vowel to get its accent.

The keyboard starts knowing nothing and learns from every word you finish. It lights up the letters likely to come next and, if you touch right on the border between two letters, picks the likelier one (you see it before lifting). If it gets it wrong, delete right away: the letter you touched comes back.

“⋯” opens modifiers, numbers and symbols. One tap on ⌘ applies to the next key; two taps lock it. Rest your left thumb on ⌘ and the right wheel shows the most used shortcuts.
